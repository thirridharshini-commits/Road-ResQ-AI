# 🚨 Road ResQ AI

### **AI-Powered Autonomous Roadside Rescue Platform**

**Tagline:** *From Breakdown to Safety.*

---

# 1. Problem Statement

Highway travel has become increasingly common, but travelers can suddenly face **vehicle breakdowns, flat tyres, dead batteries, fuel shortages, EV battery depletion, accidents, and medical emergencies**—often in remote locations or during nighttime.

The problem becomes more serious when:

* 🛣️ The nearest mechanic is several kilometers away.
* 🌙 A person is stranded alone at night.
* 👩 Women travelers face additional safety concerns.
* 📵 Mobile connectivity is weak or unavailable.
* 🗣️ The traveler and mechanic speak different languages.
* 🔧 The nearest mechanic may not have the required skills or tools.
* 💰 Stranded travelers may face uncertain or inflated prices.
* 🚗 A roadside repair may fail and require towing.
* 🚨 In an emergency, contacting the right person quickly can be difficult.

### Existing limitation

Most existing navigation and roadside-assistance solutions primarily answer:

> **“Where is the nearest service?”**

But the real question is:

> **“What exactly is happening, who is the right person to help, how quickly can they reach me, and what should happen if the first solution fails?”**

**Road ResQ AI solves this complete problem rather than simply locating a mechanic.**

---

# 2. Proposed Solution

## **Road ResQ AI**

Road ResQ AI is a **multimodal, multilingual and agentic AI-powered roadside rescue platform** that understands a traveler's emergency, diagnoses the probable vehicle problem, evaluates safety risks, identifies the most suitable **verified responder**, dispatches them, tracks the rescue, and automatically re-plans the response if the initial solution fails.

### Core workflow

**Emergency → AI Diagnosis → Risk Assessment → Smart Matching → Dispatch → Live Tracking → Rescue → Re-plan if necessary**

---

# 3. How the Application Works

### Step 1 — User reports an emergency

The traveler can report the problem through:

* Text
* Voice
* Photo
* Quick emergency buttons

Example:

> "My car stopped and smoke is coming from the front."

---

### Step 2 — AI understands the problem

The AI analyzes the user's description and/or image.

Possible classification:

> **Probable engine overheating**

It can then ask relevant questions:

> "Is the temperature warning light active?"

> "Is the vehicle safely parked on the highway shoulder?"

The AI determines:

**Problem:** Engine overheating
**Required responder:** Engine mechanic
**Priority:** High

# 4. Computer Vision

The user can upload a photograph.

### AI can identify potential issues such as:

* 🛞 Flat/damaged tyre
* 🔋 Battery-related indicators
* 🔥 Visible smoke
* 🚗 Collision damage
* 💡 Dashboard warning indicators
* ⚙️ Visible mechanical damage

Technologies:

* **YOLO**
* **OpenCV**
* **Vision-capable AI model**

The system doesn't need to claim a definitive mechanical diagnosis. It can provide a **probable issue and confidence level**, with appropriate safety instructions.

---

# 5. AI Smart Mechanic Matching

This is one of the most important AI components.

The system shouldn't simply select the closest mechanic.

It calculates a **Responder Suitability Score** using:

* Distance
* Estimated arrival time
* Availability
* Vehicle compatibility
* Problem expertise
* Required tools
* Rating
* Reliability
* Previous successful rescues
* Night availability
* Verification status
* User safety preferences

### Example

| Responder  | Distance |    ETA | Expertise | Rating | AI Score |
| ---------- | -------: | -----: | --------- | -----: | -------: |
| Mechanic A |   2.1 km |  8 min | General   |    4.2 |       76 |
| Mechanic B |   3.2 km |  9 min | Engine    |    4.8 |   **95** |
| Mechanic C |   1.5 km | 14 min | Tyres     |    4.6 |       69 |

The AI chooses **Mechanic B**, even though Mechanic A is closer.

### Possible technology

**XGBoost / LightGBM / Learning-to-Rank**

For the hackathon, you can initially use a scoring model and explain that it can later be trained using historical rescue data.

* Name
* Photo
* Verification status
* Experience
* Specialization
* Vehicle types supported
* Available tools
* Service radius

* Current availability
* Night-service availability

Responders should pass the platform's verification process before receiving requests.

# 7. 👩 Women Safe Mode

This should be one of your signature features.

When a woman is stranded at night, Road ResQ AI can activate:

## **Women Safe Rescue Mode**

The user can choose safety preferences such as:

* Verified responders only
* Highly rated responders
* Female responder preference, where available
* Registered service-center preference
* Trusted-contact sharing
* Enhanced live tracking

The AI then incorporates these preferences into responder selection.

### Example

**12:30 AM — Woman stranded on highway**

The system detects:

> 🌙 Night-time roadside emergency

It prioritizes eligible verified responders according to her safety preferences and displays:

**Responder identity → Verification → Rating → ETA → Live location**

This makes safety a **core part of dispatch**, not an afterthought.

# 9. 📵 Low-Network Mode

This is a major differentiator.

Highways can have weak network coverage, so the app should not depend completely on continuous high-speed internet.

## Road ResQ Low-Network Mode

The application can maintain locally:

* Essential maps
* Highway information
* Emergency instructions
* User vehicle information
* Emergency contacts
* Previously synchronized responder information
* Language packs

### Connectivity levels

🟢 **Strong network**

Full AI + maps + live tracking

🟡 **Weak network**

Reduced data + cached maps + low-bandwidth communication

🔴 **Very poor/no data**

Local emergency information + GPS acquisition + SMS fallback concept

### Example

The app could send a compact emergency message such as:

> `ROAD RESQ SOS | LOCATION | BREAKDOWN | USER ID`

This makes your solution particularly relevant to **remote highways**.

# 14. 🔄 Agentic AI Rescue Re-planning

This is the feature I would emphasize most for an **Agentic AI hackathon**.

Imagine:

### Situation

Mechanic reaches the vehicle.

Mechanic reports:

> "The engine cannot be repaired roadside."

Instead of ending the request:

### AI automatically reasons:

**Repair failed → Towing required**

Then:

1. Finds suitable towing provider.
2. Calculates fastest route.
3. Finds compatible service center.
4. Updates the user.
5. Tracks towing vehicle.
6. Continues monitoring the rescue.

### Rescue chain

```text
Breakdown
   ↓
AI Diagnosis
   ↓
Mechanic
   ↓
Repair Failed
   ↓
AI Re-planning
   ↓
Towing
   ↓
Service Center
   ↓
Safe Destination
```

That's what makes Road ResQ **agentic**, rather than simply an AI chatbot.
